About & Support

A guide to the pages on the website and the tools available on each one.

About us

BillionairesXchange brings market information, paper-trading tools, a trading bot interface, treasury and investment features, and community services together in one website.

This directory describes the website as it is currently presented. Some financial actions are demonstrations or paper simulations: paper balances and trades are not real funds, and the profile page states that payment processing and bank authorization are not connected. Market information and strategies do not guarantee returns.

Pages and what they do

Here is a guide to the main pages and the tools each one provides.

Home

Introduces the platform, highlights companies available for investment, and displays a live cryptocurrency market ticker. Select a company card to reveal or hide its profile details.

Trade Center

Browse crypto and other market listings with search and category filters, market summaries, and a watchlist. The chart supports market and time selection, chart tools, and chart-based order entry. Users can set up paper trades, review open positions and trade history, and manage the Trade Paper Balance. Deposit and profit-transfer controls are paper-trading simulations, not bank transfers.

Trading Bot

Provides favorite-coin monitoring, market analysis, opportunity scans, paper-trading strategies, and controls for coin, duration, and manual paper orders. Configure risk limits and an account profit goal, then review the bot’s chart, paper performance, positions, and recent trades. Market momentum is informational and does not guarantee returns.

Treasury & Wallet

Displays crypto holdings, portfolio value, 24-hour change, asset count, and a preferred display currency. Its wallet interface groups deposit, withdrawal, transfer, send, bill payment, vault, insurance, mortgage, ATM-finding, payment-method, card-request, and investment options. These screens do not by themselves confirm that a real payment or bank connection is available or completed.

Investment Center

Organizes investment choices for companies, coins, stocks, bonds, and certificates of deposit. Company features include reviewing or registering a company, entering investor details, signing or generating agreement documents, and sending signed agreements to the Community Inbox. Displayed choices do not guarantee an investment transaction.

Create Your Own Coin

Collects a coin name, symbol, website, optional image, and publishing fee for a market listing. It also provides a custom-coin chart, chart timeframes and tools, and a market-order interface for buying or selling a created coin.

Shop

Browse gadgets, gaming, Bitcoin miners, grocery stores, and gift cards. Gift-card listings can be browsed or published with a face value, asking price, description, and optional image. Publishing a listing does not process a payment automatically.

Account Verification

Provides account sign-up and sign-in flows and collects account, trading-experience, funding, risk, and identity-verification information. Submitting identity documents supports onboarding and review but does not confirm approval.

Profile

Shows signed-in account details and tools for saved payment methods, card controls, recorded activity and paper-trading history, account-closure requests, and community posts. Payment processing and bank authorization are not connected; submitting a closure request does not automatically delete an account.

Settings

Choose and save the site’s Black and Yellow, White and Yellow, or Live Pixel theme. Your selection is stored in this browser and used across the site. A live video background for the pixel theme may be added later.

Billionaires World

Provides private-community tools to search for users, create and view groups, chat with a selected user, and attach photos or videos to messages. Send-payment and request-payment controls do not by themselves confirm a real funds transfer.

Live Feed

Displays community posts and lets signed-in users compose posts with text and optional photos or videos.

Community Profile

Displays a community member’s public profile information and their community posts.

Community Inbox

Shows messages and signed investment agreements delivered to the account, including contract details, a document preview, and a PDF download when one is available.

Request an ATM Card

Preview available metal or plastic card designs, provide a displayed name and optional design image or notes, submit a card request, and view saved requests. Requests are subject to review and approval; submitting one does not confirm that a card has been issued.

About & Support

Explains the platform, describes its pages and support departments, and provides guidance for choosing the right department for a question.

Standalone Live Crypto Chart

An auxiliary chart page with a selector for BTC/USDT, ETH/USDT, and BNB/USDT. It is separate from the main site navigation.

Support departments

Choose the department that best matches the issue you need help with.

Account Department

Helps with setting up an account, finding or recovering access to an existing account, updating account details, and requesting account removal or closure. Account removal requests may require identity checks and review; submitting a request does not necessarily delete an account immediately.

Credit Card Department

Helps with adding or managing a credit or debit card, requesting a new card, choosing or changing a card design, and reporting a lost or stolen card. If a card is stolen, contact your card issuer promptly to secure it. Never share a full card number, PIN, password, or security code in a support request.

Finance Department

Helps with questions about deposits, withdrawals, transfers between accounts, and sending funds. The website currently includes simulated paper-trading balances, and its profile page states that payment processing and bank authorization are not connected; check whether a transaction is supported before relying on it as a real-money service.

Pay Bills Department

Helps with bill-payment questions and the website’s insurance and mortgage areas, including understanding the displayed options and reporting a problem with a submitted request. Availability, eligibility, payment status, and terms should be confirmed through the relevant provider; seeing an option in the interface does not confirm that a bill or policy has been paid or activated.

Security Department

Helps with account-security concerns, suspected fraud, unauthorized activity, and questions about encryption and the Fraud Protection Vault. The planned hosting setup uses managed HTTPS/TLS for the Firebase-hosted website and the Render-hosted API, with HTTP Strict Transport Security configured. These protections take effect only after both services are deployed and their HTTPS domains are active. TLS protects data in transit; it does not by itself encrypt stored data or prove a security certification. Avoid sharing passwords, verification codes, or private financial details.
