About & Support

A guide to the pages on the website and the tools available on each one.

About us

BillionairesXchange brings market information, paper-trading tools, a trading bot interface, treasury and investment features, and community services together in one website.

This directory describes the website as it is currently presented. Some financial actions are demonstrations or paper simulations: paper balances and trades are not real funds, and the profile page states that payment processing and bank authorization are not connected. Market information and strategies do not guarantee returns.

Pages and what they do

The page names below are a directory, not buttons or links.

Home — index.html

Introduces the platform and highlights companies available for investment. Company cards show an icon and name first; selecting a card reveals or hides its profile details.

Trade Center — trade.html

Shows crypto and other market listings with search and category filters, market summaries, and a watchlist. The chart supports market and time selection, chart tools, and chart-based order entry. Users can set up a paper trade with quantity and risk levels, review open positions and trade history, and manage the Trade Paper Balance. Deposit and profit-transfer controls on this page are paper-trading simulations, not bank transfers.

Trading Bot — bot-trading.html

Provides favorite-coin monitoring, market analysis and opportunity scans, paper-trading strategies, and controls for coin, duration, and manual paper orders. Separate settings let users configure risk limits and an account profit goal. The page displays the bot configuration, market chart, paper performance, positions, and recent trades, and shares paper-balance information with Trade. Market momentum is informational, not a profit guarantee.

Treasury & Wallet — treasury.html

Displays crypto holdings, portfolio value, 24-hour change, asset count, and a preferred display currency. Its wallet interface groups deposit, withdrawal, transfer, send, bill payment, vault, insurance, mortgage, ATM-finding, payment-method, card-request, and investment options. These screens do not by themselves confirm that a real payment or bank connection is available or completed.

Investment Center — investment.html

Organizes investment choices for companies, coins, stocks, bonds, and certificates of deposit. Company features include reviewing company profiles, registering a company, entering investor details, signing or generating agreement documents, and sending signed agreements to the Community Inbox. Availability and financial execution depend on connected services; a displayed choice is not a guarantee of an investment transaction.

Create Your Own Coin — create-coin.html

Collects a coin name, symbol, website, optional image, and publishing fee for a market listing. It also provides a custom-coin chart, chart timeframes and tools, and a market-order interface for buying or selling a created coin.

Shop — shop.html

Groups products into gadgets, gaming, Bitcoin miners, and gift cards. Users can browse category information and gift-card listings, switch between buying and selling gift cards, and publish a listing with its face value, asking price, description, and optional image. Listings do not process a payment automatically.

Account Verification — verification.html

Provides account sign-up and sign-in flows and collects account, trading-experience, funding, risk, and identity-verification information. Identity-document submission supports the onboarding and review flow; completing a form should not be taken as confirmation of approval.

Profile (account) — profile.html

Shows the signed-in account profile and details. Account tools include saved payment-method management, card freeze controls, recorded activity and paper-trading history, an account-closure request, and photo or video posts for the community. Its page explains that payment processing and bank authorization are not connected and that submitting a closure request does not automatically delete the account.

Billionaires World — billionaires-world.html

Provides private-community tools to search for users, create and view groups, chat with a selected user, and attach a photo or video to a message. It also presents send-payment and request-payment controls; the interface alone does not confirm a real funds transfer.

Live Feed — live-feed.html

Displays community posts and lets signed-in users compose posts with text and optional photo or video. Posts are associated with community accounts.

Community Profile — user-profile.html

Displays a community member’s public profile information and their community posts.

Community Inbox — community-inbox.html

Shows messages and signed investment agreements delivered to the account, including contract details, a document preview, and a PDF download when one is available.

Request an ATM Card — request-card.html

Lets users preview available metal or plastic card designs, provide the displayed name and optional design image or notes, submit a card request, and view their saved requests. Requests are subject to review and approval; a request is not confirmation that a card has been issued.

About & Support — about-support.html

This page describes the platform and lists its pages and functions. The directory is plain text rather than a collection of page-link buttons.

Standalone Live Crypto Chart — files if needed/index.html

An auxiliary chart page that loads a separate chart script and provides a selector for BTC/USDT, ETH/USDT, and BNB/USDT. It is separate from the main site navigation.

Support departments

Choose the department that best matches the issue you need help with.

Account Department

Helps with setting up an account, finding or recovering access to an existing account, updating account details, and requesting account removal or closure. Account removal requests may require identity checks and review; submitting a request does not necessarily delete an account immediately.

Credit Card Department

Helps with adding or managing a credit or debit card, requesting a new card, choosing or changing a card design, and reporting a lost or stolen card. If a card is stolen, contact your card issuer promptly to secure it. Never share a full card number, PIN, password, or security code in a support request.

Finance Department

Helps with questions about deposits, withdrawals, transfers between accounts, and sending funds. The website currently includes simulated paper-trading balances, and its profile page states that payment processing and bank authorization are not connected; check whether a transaction is supported before relying on it as a real-money service.

Pay Bills Department

Helps with bill-payment questions and the website’s insurance and mortgage areas, including understanding the displayed options and reporting a problem with a submitted request. Availability, eligibility, payment status, and terms should be confirmed through the relevant provider; seeing an option in the interface does not confirm that a bill or policy has been paid or activated.

Security Department

Helps with account-security concerns, suspected fraud, unauthorized activity, and questions about encryption and the Fraud Protection Vault. The planned hosting setup uses managed HTTPS/TLS for the Firebase-hosted website and the Render-hosted API, with HTTP Strict Transport Security configured. These protections take effect only after both services are deployed and their HTTPS domains are active. TLS protects data in transit; it does not by itself encrypt stored data or prove a security certification. Avoid sharing passwords, verification codes, or private financial details.
