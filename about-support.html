Guides & security

Treasury Security Features

🔐

Encrypted Local Recovery

The BIP-39 phrase is encrypted in this browser with AES-GCM and a password-derived key. Back it up offline.

🛡️

You Control Your Keys

Private keys are derived in your browser and are not sent to BillionairesXchange. Anyone with your recovery phrase can control your assets.

⏱️

Automatic Wallet Lock

The wallet locks after 15 minutes of inactivity. A revealed backup phrase is hidden automatically after 60 seconds.

✍️

Local Transaction Signing

Transactions are signed in your browser and broadcast through public blockchain services after your confirmation.

📊

On-Chain Balances

Native BTC, ETH, BNB, POL, and SOL balances are refreshed from public blockchain services.

🌍

Global Reach

Payment options are presented for supported locations and currencies.

💳

Multiple Payment Methods

Deposit and withdraw using bank transfers, cards, crypto, and other payment options.

These are browser-wallet safeguards, not exchange custody, account insurance, or a guarantee against compromised devices. Secure your device and keep your recovery phrase offline.

How Bot Trading Works

1. Configure Settings

Select your preferred coin, set your risk level, and choose a trading strategy that suits your investment goals.

2. Set Risk Management

Define your stop-loss to limit losses and take-profit to secure gains. The bot will automatically close positions at these levels.

3. Choose Duration

Select how long the bot should trade - from quick 1-hour sessions to continuous 24/7 automated trading.

4. Monitor & Adjust

Watch your bot in real-time, receive notifications on trades, and adjust settings as market conditions change.

How to Create Your Coin

1. Enter Coin Details

Choose a unique name, symbol, and website URL for your cryptocurrency. This information will be displayed on the marketplace.

2. Upload Coin Image

Add a professional logo or icon for your coin. This helps users identify and remember your coin in the marketplace.

3. Set Initial Supply

Choose the number of tokens minted once to your wallet. The fixed supply cannot be increased or minted again.

4. Deploy the ERC-20

Choose Ethereum, BNB Smart Chain, or Polygon PoS, then approve the deployment in your wallet and pay that network's gas fee. Your contract is saved after confirmation.
