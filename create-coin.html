    How to Create Your Coin

    1. Enter Coin Details

    Choose a unique name, symbol, and website URL for your cryptocurrency. This information will be displayed on the marketplace.

    2. Upload Coin Image

    Add a professional logo or icon for your coin. This helps users identify and remember your coin in the marketplace.

    3. Set Initial Supply

    Choose the number of tokens minted once to your wallet. The fixed supply cannot be increased or minted again.

    4. Deploy the ERC-20

    Approve the Polygon transaction in your wallet and pay its network gas fee. Your token contract address is saved after confirmation.
